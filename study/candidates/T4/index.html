Our services

Everything below is available at all three Fernlea sites, with extended hours at the Riverside clinic.

General check-ups

Blood pressure, weight, general wellbeing and a friendly chat about anything on your mind.

Vaccinations

Seasonal and travel vaccines, with a nurse to explain what to expect and answer your questions.

Long-term care

Regular reviews for asthma, diabetes and other long-term conditions, planned around your routine.

Meet the team

Our small team of doctors, nurses and receptionists have worked together for many years. We try to make sure you see the same familiar face whenever possible.

Preparing for your visit

Before you arrive

Make a short list of your questions and symptoms, and bring any medicines you currently take in their original packaging.

On the day

Please arrive ten minutes early so reception can confirm your details. Step-free access is available at every site.

If you need to cancel

Let us know as early as you can so someone else can use your slot. You can reply to your confirmation email.

Can a friend or relative come with me?

Yes. You are welcome to bring someone for support, and we can arrange an interpreter on request.

What if my concern is urgent?

This form is for non-urgent requests. For anything urgent, call the clinic directly or contact your local emergency service.
