Year 9 timetable for the autumn term

This page has all the information that Year 9 students and their families need for the first half of term: the weekly timetable, the dates that matter, where each room is, and a form for asking about a change.

Useful information

Students should arrive by 08:40 and have their planner out on the desk for registration. Equipment for PE and Art is listed on the department pages, and lockers can be rented from the school office for the year.

If a lesson moves, a notice appears on the screens by the main entrance and on this page by 08:00 on the day. Send questions about a subject to the head of department, and send urgent questions to the school office.

Where to find your rooms

Science block (S1 to S4)

Ground floor of the east wing. Students wait in the corridor until the teacher opens the lab, and coats stay on the hooks outside the room.

Humanities (H1 to H4, G1)

First floor of the main building, reached by the central staircase. The lift is next to the library for anyone who needs it.

Arts (A1, MU, Hall)

Across the courtyard in the glass building. The hall also hosts PE on wet days, so check the screens for changes at break time.

Ask about a change

If your timetable has a clash, a missing lesson or a room that seems wrong, tell us here. The timetabling team replies within two school days and will never change a lesson without telling a parent or carer first.

For urgent matters on the day, speak to your form tutor or call the office.

A parent or carer email is fine.
