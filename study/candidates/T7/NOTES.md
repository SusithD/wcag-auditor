Context: Larkfield High School, fictional Year 9 timetable and term dates page (navy and yellow, table heavy).
Seeded defect (SC 2.4.13): the global focus style is a 1 px solid #a3adbb outline with no offset, about 2.3:1 on white, so it is visible (2.4.7 passes) but under 2 px and under 3:1.
Location: the `form :focus-visible{outline:1px solid #a3adbb;outline-offset:0}` rule near the top of the stylesheet (restricted to form controls in revision 2).
Minimal fix: `:focus-visible{outline:3px solid #0b3d91;outline-offset:2px}` (or 2 px at 3:1 or more).
Risk: the footer sits on a dark background where the pale outline looks stronger; the fix colour should be checked there (footer links could get an outline of #ffd966). The timetable scroll region is a tab stop and also gets the thin outline.
Revision 2 (after review): the pale 1 px #a3adbb outline (2.27:1 on white) now applies only to form controls (`form :focus-visible`); nav, links, the table scroll region, the skip link and buttons outside the form have a compliant 3 px #0b3d91 outline (10.0:1), and the dark footer has its own `footer :focus-visible{outline-color:#ffd966}` (11.7:1 on #14213d) in the shipped CSS so the expected fix cannot create a new failure there. The .skip link is high contrast. This page also fails 1.4.11 for the focus indicator; the expected fix is a 2 px or thicker solid outline with at least 3:1 against all adjacent backgrounds (white form and dark footer), e.g. `form :focus-visible{outline:3px solid #0b3d91;outline-offset:2px}`.
