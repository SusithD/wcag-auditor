Autumn term, six Tuesday evenings

Make something by hand this autumn

Thistlebank is a small, friendly pottery studio above the old grain store in the fictional village of Thistlebank. Our evening classes run for six weeks, and no experience is needed and all clay, glazes and firing included in the price. You take home finished pieces that are fired, glazed and ready to use.

Choose your class

Every class is capped at eight people so the tutor can sit with you at the wheel or bench. Choose the one you like best. Your skill does not matter. Beginners are very welcome in all of them.

A relaxed, friendly studio

Thistlebank started when two friends bought a used kiln and some old wooden benches. Today the studio has six electric wheels, a long glazing table and a corner with armchairs where people drink tea while the kiln is on.

We keep classes small and the music quiet. Aprons are provided, but please wear clothes that can get dirty with wet clay. You do not need to shower afterwards, but please wash your hands.

Read our frequently asked questions if you are wondering about accessibility, parking or what happens to your pots between weeks.

How a Tuesday evening works

  1. Arrive from 17:45, choose an apron and a stool and say hello.
  2. A short demonstration from your tutor, about fifteen minutes.
  3. Time at the wheel or bench, with a tutor always close by.
  4. Tidy-up together, then label your pieces for the shelf.
  5. Tea and biscuits while we plan next week.

Common questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Most of our students have never touched clay. Your tutor will teach at the speed of the slowest learner, and in week one that is usually everyone.

Is the studio step-free?

There is a ramp at the side door and a lift to the first floor. One wheel is on a height-adjustable stand, so please mention it in your booking notes.

What happens to my pots between weeks?

They rest on the labelled shelf, wrapped in plastic so they stay workable. Anything finished is bisque-fired in week five and glaze-fired after the last class.

Can I cancel?

Yes, up to seven days before the first class for a full refund. After that we can usually move you to the next term.
