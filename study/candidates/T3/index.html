Kitchen tips

Season in layers

Add a little salt early and the rest at the end. Tasting as you go gives you far more control than a single big pinch.

Rest your dough

Cookie dough chilled for thirty minutes spreads less and tastes deeper. Even a short rest makes a visible difference.

Freeze in portions

Stews and soups freeze well. Cool them fully, then store in single servings so lunch is always a quick defrost away.

Can I swap butter for oil? In most baking you can use a mild oil at about three quarters of the butter weight. Flavour will be lighter.

How do I scale a recipe up? Multiply the ingredients, but increase seasoning and cooking time more gently and taste before adding more.
