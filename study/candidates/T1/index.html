Our story

Marlowe Green Library opened in a converted tram shed in 1962 with two thousand donated books and a single librarian. Today it holds more than forty thousand items and welcomes about three hundred visitors every day.

The building was refurbished last spring with a step-free entrance, a quiet study room and a bright new children's area. Every change was shaped by suggestions from neighbours, school groups and the volunteer committee, who still meet on the first Monday of each month in the reading room to plan what happens next.

Find us at 14 Tramshed Lane, Marlowe Green. Buses 12 and 31 stop outside, and there is free bicycle parking at the side entrance.

Can I return books when the library is closed?

Yes. The return slot beside the main door is open day and night.

Is there a charge for printing?

Black and white pages cost 10 pence each. Colour pages cost 30 pence each.

Do you offer large print or audio books?

Both. Ask at the desk and we will help you find something to suit.
