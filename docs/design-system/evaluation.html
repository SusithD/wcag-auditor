Question 1: does the hybrid find failures that rules cannot?

Rule-only cannot decide five of the eight criteria on real pages. The hybrid finds failures on four of them, with recall from 0.438 to 1.000, and none of the 15 real 2.4.12 failures. It also raises false alarms where rule-only stays silent, which the paired table shows.

What this measures

  • Single-image inference, each image squashed to 576 by 576. This is not the accuracy of the tiled pipeline.
  • The split is held-out WebUI validation, not the 110-page benchmark.
  • Training and validation labels are roughly one third noisy, so these scores are a lower bound.
  • Per-class box counts for the split are not recorded in the report.

Limits

  • Label noise. A one-third noise rate (AI-assisted, 200 boxes, CI ±6.5) bounds what the detector can show. Button, input and choice had 5 boxes between them, so their noise is unmeasured.
  • Small samples. Real pages hold 11 failures for 2.4.11, 15 for 2.4.12, 34 for 2.5.8 and none for 2.5.7. Intervals under n = 30 are not informative.
  • False alarms. Hybrid precision on real pages is 0.088 for 2.4.11, 0.124 for 1.4.11 and 0.147 for 2.5.8. Real 2.4.12 recall is 0.000 (0 of 15).
  • Two headline runs. Without the detector (2026-10-10) and with it (2026-10-11) are both reported. On real pages 1.4.11 recall moves from 0.000 to 0.438, and hybrid 2.5.8 precision from 0.162 to 0.147.
  • Coverage. The tool checks 8 of the 86 WCAG 2.2 success criteria. A result is not a conformance claim.
