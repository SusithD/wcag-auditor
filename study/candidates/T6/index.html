Walk, talk and enjoy the outdoors together

Fernhill Ramblers is a friendly, volunteer-run walking club for people of every pace. We walk most Saturdays and some Wednesday evenings in summer, across moorland, river paths and gentle ridges. Membership is £5 for the year and your first walk is free.

What to bring

Footwear
Walking boots or sturdy trail shoes with a good grip. Trainers are fine only on the easy walks.
Clothing
Layers, a waterproof jacket and a warm hat. Cotton gets cold when wet, so wool or synthetics are better.
Food and drink
At least a litre of water and a packed lunch. Many people bring a hot drink in a flask.
Safety
A charged phone, any personal medication and a whistle if you have one. Leaders carry a first aid kit and a map.

About the club

We began in 1998 as six neighbours who wanted a reason to go outside on Saturdays. Today there are about ninety members, aged from sixteen to eighty-four, and a rota of twelve volunteer leaders who plan routes and check the paths in advance.

We follow the country code, leave no trace and keep groups to fifteen people, so paths stay quiet and farmers are happy to see us. We wait for everyone, and nobody is hurried.

Walking with a disability or a health condition

Tell us what would help and we will choose routes and a pace to suit you. Several of our easy walks have firm, wide paths suitable for most users. Read more on the sign-up form or ask a leader at the start.
