Wobblewheel is a friendly independent workshop for commuter, family and touring bikes. Most repairs are finished within two working days and every job comes with a free safety check.

What we fix

From a squeaky chain to a full rebuild, our mechanics have seen it all. Here are the repairs customers ask for most.

Tune-ups and gears

Indexing, cable replacement, chain and cassette fitting, and a full drivetrain clean so your gears shift smoothly again.

Wheels and tyres

Punctures, new tubes, tubeless set-up, spoke replacement and wheel truing for bent rims and loose spokes.

Brakes and safety

Pad replacement, hydraulic bleeds, cable adjustment and a lights and reflectors check before you ride away.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for punctures and quick adjustments. Bookings are recommended for services.

Do you repair electric bikes?

We can service the mechanical parts of most electric bikes. Motor and battery faults are referred to the manufacturer.

How long will you keep my bike?

We store finished bikes for up to seven days at no charge.
